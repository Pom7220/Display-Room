Central Silom Tower · RIS floor · 12 meeting rooms

Room status indicators

Available
Room is free — you can walk in or book it
In use
Meeting in progress — check in if it's yours
Coming soon
Next meeting starts within 30 minutes
Pending
Booking awaiting room owner approval (Macchiato only)

How to book a room

1

Tap "+ Book Room" at the bottom of the tablet display

2

Enter your name, email, and meeting title

3

Pick a start time and duration (30 min to 2 hours)

4

Tap "Confirm" — you'll receive an Outlook calendar invitation

⚠ Macchiato is the only room requiring owner approval. Booking will show as "Pending" until approved. All other rooms confirm instantly.

During your meeting

✓ Check in
Confirm you're using the room. Rooms auto-release after 10 min if nobody checks in.
+30m Extend
Add 30 minutes if the next slot is free.
✕ End meeting
Release the room early so others can book it.

Viewing the schedule

Tap Today or Tomorrow tabs on the display to see all meetings for the room. Tap More+ to see up to 7 days ahead.
